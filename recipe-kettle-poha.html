10-Minute Electric Kettle Masala Poha

By Aman Sharma • Updated: Today • ₹18 / plate • ★ [ADD RATING]

Living in an engineering hostel or single PG room without a stove? This easy, fragrant Masala Poha is cooked straight in your 1.5L electric kettle using residual heat — soft, warm, aromatic, and zero burned pots!

When 8:30 AM lectures loom and the hostel mess serves cold bread with watery tea, this 10-minute kettle poha is a lifesaver. Thick flattened rice (poha) needs very little heat to cook through — its magic lies in steam absorption rather than hard boiling.

By quickly tempering peanuts and mustard seeds in 1 teaspoon of oil inside the kettle base and immediately shutting off the power before adding rinsed poha, you avoid triggering smoke alarms and protect the kettle's heating coil from scorching.

Hostel Kettle Masala Poha

⚠️ Hostel Room Rule & Cooking Safety Tip

Never use metal spoons inside your electric kettle! Scratches can strip the protective steel coat and short-circuit the base. Use a silicone or wooden spoon. Always unplug the kettle before adding poha so residual heat does the cooking.

Ingredients

Tap checkbox as you prep in your room
  • 1 cup Thick Poha (flattened rice, washed & drained) ₹8
  • 2 tbsp Roasted Peanuts ₹4
  • 1/2 tsp Mustard seeds (Rai) + pinch of Hing ₹1
  • 1/4 tsp Turmeric powder (Haldi) + 1/2 tsp Salt ₹1
  • 1 Green chilli, finely chopped ₹1
  • 1 tsp Cooking oil ₹1
  • 1/2 Fresh Lemon + 1 tbsp Sev/Bhujia (optional crunch) ₹2

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Rinse the Poha: Place the thick poha in a stainless steel tea strainer or colander. Rinse under the tap for 10 seconds. Shake off water and sprinkle salt, turmeric, and a pinch of sugar. Let it rest for 2 minutes to soften.
  2. Warm the Tempering: Plug in the electric kettle. Add 1 tsp cooking oil, mustard seeds, green chilli, and peanuts. Turn on the kettle switch for approximately 40 seconds until the mustard crackles.
  3. Switch OFF & Unplug: Immediately turn the switch off and disconnect the plug from the wall socket. Do not let the kettle stay powered on with dry contents.
  4. Combine & Steam: Tip the softened poha into the kettle. Gently mix with a wooden or silicone spoon so the golden turmeric oil coats the grains.
  5. Rest under Lid: Close the kettle lid tightly and allow the trapped steam to warm the poha for 3 to 4 minutes.
  6. Serve: Scoop the poha into your bowl. Squeeze fresh lemon juice over the top, sprinkle crispy sev, and enjoy a steaming hot homemade breakfast!
